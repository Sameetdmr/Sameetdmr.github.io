About Me

Engineering Mobile
Experiences at Scale

I'm a self-taught Mobile Software Engineer based in Istanbul, currently building mobile applications at LC Waikiki — one of Turkey's largest retail brands. My journey started with curiosity and evolved into a professional engineering career over 4+ years.

My core expertise is Flutter & Dart for cross-platform development, with deep knowledge of hardware integrations (Bluetooth, barcode scanners, ZPL printers) and real-time communication via SignalR. I've led and delivered projects used by thousands of customers and store staff across Turkey.

Outside of work, I publish open source Flutter packages, develop indie apps on the App Store, and write technical articles to share knowledge with the developer community.
