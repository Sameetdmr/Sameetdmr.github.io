About

Most of my work sits where a mobile app meets a physical device. At LC Waikiki I digitalize in-store operations: the apps staff carry on hand terminals, the kiosks customers use, and the printers and scanners both depend on.

That means Bluetooth serial connections, ZPL for Zebra label printers, Point Mobile PDA scanners, offline-first storage in SQLite and real-time sync over SignalR, all built on Clean Architecture and SOLID principles.

Outside work I design, build and publish my own apps on the App Store, and I maintain a Zebra printer package on pub.dev that other Flutter developers use.
